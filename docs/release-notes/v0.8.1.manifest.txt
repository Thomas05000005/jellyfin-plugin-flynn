The two music audits now share one pass over the library instead of walking it once each. Audio
items carry no source-serialisation attribute, so every track row costs a JSON deserialisation of
its data blob -- on a 223 000-track library that was 447 000 of them a night to answer two
questions. More to the point, the nightly task never consulted the module toggles at all: a music
module you had switched off still paid for its own full walk every night to produce a figure you
would never be shown. It now collects only what is enabled, and when both music modules are off it
reads nothing whatsoever, not even the list of libraries. Sharing a pass could have cost the two
audits their independence, so it does not: a collector that fails is dropped from the walk and the
others finish. Requires Jellyfin 12.0 or later.
