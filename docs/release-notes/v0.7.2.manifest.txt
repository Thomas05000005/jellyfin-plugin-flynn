Stops the capacity forecast counting readings taken minutes apart as if they described a day. The
task can be run by hand next to its schedule, and normalising a small change over a short gap
produces an enormous daily rate -- on the server this was written for, a 1.4 GB move measured over
three hours normalised to eleven gigabytes a day. The median absorbed it and still carried it,
putting the answer 27 percent away from what the nightly readings alone say. Gaps shorter than six
hours are now discarded, unless there are too few long ones to do without them, because a server
whose task only ever runs by hand still deserves an answer. Requires Jellyfin 12.0 or later.
