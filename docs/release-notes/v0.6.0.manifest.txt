The issue inbox becomes usable. Dismiss, snooze for a week, or bring back anything hidden -- and
the withheld ones are now listed rather than only counted, because a count with no way to see what
it counts is a slightly better blind spot rather than a way back. The three endpoints already
existed and could not work: an issue fingerprint contains slashes, so the route segment they were
declared on could never match one. Also fixes eleven French strings that shipped without their
accents, and shows what the CPU percentage is a share of, since eighty percent of a quota means
throttling is imminent while eighty percent of a machine only means busy. Requires Jellyfin 12.0 or
later.
