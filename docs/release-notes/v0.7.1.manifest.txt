Stops the Music card averaging across libraries that are in different states. On the server this
was written for, one music library sat at 100% because its loudness scan had been switched on and
the other at 0% because it had not; the card showed their average, 11%, a figure that described
neither library and pointed at nothing to do. It now names the library that needs attention and
quotes its own number, and says plainly how the rest of the server looks so that a 0% reads as the
setting it is rather than as a fault. Requires Jellyfin 12.0 or later.
